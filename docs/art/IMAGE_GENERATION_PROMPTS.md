# Internal image generation prompts

Submit these prompts to the approved illustration production workflow only. All images are pending; these specifications are not evidence of generated or approved assets. Generate each asset individually for clean composition. Reuse approved enemy references for later poses.

## Shared style prefix

Original painterly dark fantasy illustration for Deckbound, the Ashen Crypt. Stylized hand-painted brushwork, strong readable silhouette, weathered basalt and aged bronze, charcoal and slate shadows, warm torch key light and a subtle cool rim, restrained atmospheric ash. Cohesive premium indie card-game presentation. No photorealism, no glossy 3D render, no modern objects, no recognizable existing franchise designs. No text, letters, numbers, watermark, UI, or card frame. Keep the focal subject within the central 70 percent with a 10 percent crop safety margin.

Append the following asset-specific direction to the shared prefix. For transparent assets explicitly request transparent_background=true; do not substitute a painted checkerboard. Card art must never contain card names, rules, numbers, or frames.
