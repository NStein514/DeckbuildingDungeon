# Archetype fusion expansion

The shrine offers seventeen recipes and the catalog contains 62 illustrated cards. Ten recipes combine deck collectibles into specialized fusion results. Fuse either order; both source instances (including their upgrades) are consumed. Results start unupgraded and can be upgraded once. Fusion results never appear in shops or battle rewards. One fusion ends the shrine visit.

| Sources | Result | Energy | Effects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Measured Strike + Lantern Watch | Dawnward | 1 | 6 damage. 5 block. Draw 1. |
| Stormglass Focus + Winter Circuit | Storm Prism | 1 | 4 Shock, 2 Chill. Draw 1. Exhaust. |
| Patient Fang + Wasting Venom | Widow's Promise | 1 | 6 damage. 5 Poison. Retain. |
| Graven Resolve + Ossuary Ward | Ancient Bastion | 2 | 12 block. 2 Strength. Retain. Exhaust. |
| Phoenix Brand + Cinder Insight | Solar Script | 2 | 9 damage. 5 Burn. Draw 1. Exhaust. |

Dawnward combines offense, block and draw. Storm Prism buys time with Chill while preparing lightning damage. Widow’s Promise retains a repeatable poison attack. Ancient Bastion gives lasting Strength and a large retained ward but exhausts after use. Solar Script is a one-use damage/Burn/draw spell. All use existing upgrade, targeting, and status rules.

Existing saves remain supported. Card pools are unchanged because all five results are fusion-only. Recipes are visible in Combos & fusions and appear as shortcuts at a shrine when the deck holds both sources.

## Advanced fusions

| Sources | Result | Energy | Effects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trail Rations + Lantern Watch | Trailkeeper | 1 | Heal 4. Gain 6 block. Draw 1. Exhaust. |
| Forked Current + Thunderhead | Tempest Fork | 2 | Deal 5 damage twice. Apply 3 Shock. |
| Bitter Bloom + Serpent Step | Noxious Covenant | 1 | Apply 5 Poison and 2 Weak. Retain. Exhaust. |
| Grave Oath + Renewal | Gravebloom | 1 | Gain 8 block and 4 Regeneration. Exhaust. |
| Flashfire + Ash Surge | Cinderwake | 0 | Deal 4 damage. Apply 3 Burn and 1 Vulnerable. Exhaust. |

Trailkeeper combines recovery, defense, and replacement draw for longer descents. Tempest Fork consumes existing Shock on its first lightning hit, then leaves new Shock for a subsequent lightning play. Noxious Covenant can wait in hand for an enemy's dangerous turn; its cheaper cost and Retain replace Serpent Step's block. Gravebloom blocks while regeneration heals at the end of consecutive turns. Cinderwake is a free opener: Vulnerable benefits later attacks, not its own initial hit.

These results are fusion-only. Existing runs retain their reward/shop pools, and no new run is required to use recipes when both source cards are owned. Save content versions advance to run 19 and profile 9 while older saves remain readable.
