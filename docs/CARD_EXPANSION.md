# Archetype support cards

Twenty-five collectible cards expand the five build themes. All builds can use them; the camp recommendations describe synergies, not restrictions. Starting decks and unlocks remain unchanged. New runs find these cards in rewards and merchant stock. Existing runs keep their previous pool through later descents to preserve seeded offers and sold stock.

| Build | Card | Energy | Rarity | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wanderer | Trailblazer Cut | 1 | uncommon | Deal 4 damage. Draw 1 card. |
| Wanderer | Lantern Watch | 1 | uncommon | Gain 4 block. Draw 1 card. Exhaust. |
| Stormweaver | Charged Aegis | 1 | uncommon | Gain 5 block. Apply 2 Shock. |
| Stormweaver | Forked Current | 1 | rare | Deal 3 damage twice. Lightning consumes Shock for bonus damage. |
| Venomblade | Wasting Venom | 1 | uncommon | Apply 3 Poison and 1 Weak. |
| Venomblade | Serpent Step | 0 | uncommon | Gain 3 block. Retain. Exhaust. |
| Gravewarden | Grave Oath | 1 | uncommon | Gain 5 block and 1 Regeneration. Exhaust. |
| Gravewarden | Tomb Seal | 2 | uncommon | Gain 8 block. Apply 2 Weak. |
| Embercaller | Ember Veil | 1 | uncommon | Gain 5 block. Apply 2 Burn. |
| Embercaller | Ash Surge | 1 | rare | Apply 4 Burn and 1 Vulnerable. Exhaust. |

Healing support and zero-cost retained evasion exhaust to limit repeated use. Lightning consumes existing Shock on the first damaging hit; Forked Current then resolves its second hit normally. All cards use shared upgrade rules: +2 to damage/block/heal and +1 to status/draw amounts, with costs and keywords preserved.

## Tactical additions

These five cards are available in runs started with this update. Earlier runs keep their existing pool, including runs started with the first ten-card expansion.

| Build | Card | Energy | Rarity | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wanderer | Measured Strike | 1 | uncommon | Deal 5 damage. Gain 3 block. |
| Stormweaver | Stormglass Focus | 0 | rare | Apply 3 Shock. Draw 1 card. Exhaust. |
| Venomblade | Patient Fang | 1 | uncommon | Deal 4 damage. Apply 2 Poison. Retain. |
| Gravewarden | Crypt Reprisal | 2 | uncommon | Deal 8 damage. Gain 6 block. |
| Embercaller | Phoenix Brand | 2 | rare | Deal 7 damage. Apply 4 Burn. Exhaust. |

Stormglass Focus is a one-use setup spell that replaces itself when cards remain to draw. Patient Fang can wait in hand for a useful opening. The two guarded attacks trade energy for offense and protection together; Phoenix Brand spends two energy on immediate and delayed damage but exhausts. These use the same upgrade rules described above.

## Endurance additions

Available in new runs started with this update. Earlier runs preserve their original pool. All builds can collect these cards.

| Build | Card | Energy | Rarity | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wanderer | Trail Rations | 1 | uncommon | Restore 3 health. Draw 1 card. Exhaust. |
| Stormweaver | Winter Circuit | 1 | uncommon | Apply 2 Shock and 1 Chill. |
| Venomblade | Nightshade Mantle | 1 | uncommon | Gain 4 block. Apply 2 Poison. |
| Gravewarden | Graven Resolve | 2 | uncommon | Gain 6 block and 1 Strength. Exhaust. |
| Embercaller | Cinder Insight | 1 | uncommon | Apply 2 Burn. Draw 1 card. |

Trail Rations exhausts to limit repeat healing. Winter Circuit prepares a lightning hit while Chill reduces the enemy’s next attack turn. Nightshade Mantle combines immediate protection with delayed poison damage. Graven Resolve grants encounter-long Strength but exhausts; its two-energy cost limits the attacks that can follow immediately. Cinder Insight applies Burn while drawing another option. Normal upgrades increase healing/block by 2 and status/draw amounts by 1.

## Initiative additions

Available in runs started with this update, across all builds. Earlier adventures preserve their saved card pools.

| Build | Card | Energy | Rarity | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wanderer | Opening Gambit | 0 | rare | Deal 2 damage. Apply 1 Vulnerable. Exhaust. |
| Stormweaver | Thunderhead | 2 | uncommon | Deal 9 damage. Apply 2 Shock. |
| Venomblade | Bitter Bloom | 2 | uncommon | Apply 4 Poison and 2 Weak. Exhaust. |
| Gravewarden | Ossuary Ward | 1 | uncommon | Gain 7 block. Retain. Exhaust. |
| Embercaller | Flashfire | 0 | rare | Deal 3 damage. Apply 1 Burn. Exhaust. |

Opening Gambit applies Vulnerable after its own hit, setting up subsequent attacks. Thunderhead consumes existing Shock before applying fresh stacks. Bitter Bloom delays damage while weakening incoming attacks. Ossuary Ward can wait in hand but exhausts when used. Flashfire is a zero-energy burst that also exhausts, preventing repeated free plays from a single copy. Normal upgrades add 2 damage/block and 1 to status amounts without changing cost or keywords.
